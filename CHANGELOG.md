# Changelog

## v0.1.0

First release.

- `/zt` shows the area types (Desert, Forest & Grassland, Wasteland, Haunted, Swamp and more) for where you're standing, including towns inside zones (Everlook, Gadgetzan, Booty Bay), dungeons and raids.
- When you enter a new zone, its area types pop up on screen under the zone name, in the same style as the game's zone text (Grassland in green, Desert in gold, Wasteland in brown, Haunted in purple). Several types share one line.
- Minimap button with ZoneType's own map-pin icon: left-click unlocks the on-screen text so you can move it (left-click again to lock), right-click opens settings, drag to move the button.
- Settings (Esc > Options > AddOns > ZoneType, or right-click the minimap button): turn the pop-up on/off, text size, which area types pop up (Grassland, Desert, Wasteland and Haunted by default), the chat message, and the minimap button.
- While unlocked, drag the blue box or type an exact X / Y position under it. Center puts it in the middle left-to-right; Lock saves it.
- Optional chat message when you move into a place with different area types.
- `/zt list <type>` lists every place of a type.
- Commands: `/zt settings`, `/zt unlock`, `/zt lock`, `/zt pos <x> <y>`, `/zt pos center`, `/zt resetpos`, `/zt flash`, `/zt flash test`, `/zt auto`, `/zt minimap`, `/zt where`, `/zt test`.
